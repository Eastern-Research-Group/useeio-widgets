Glossary

Definitions used in this tool. Placeholder text; replace with final copy as indicators and methods are finalized.

Terms

Point of Consumption
Placeholder: perspective attributing impacts to where products and services are consumed (final demand), rather than tracing only upstream supply-chain stages.
Supply Chain
Placeholder: the network of sector-to-sector purchases and processes that deliver goods and services; indirect effects often arise from this network.
Impact intensity
Placeholder: impact per unit of economic activity or per purchase (e.g., embodied impact per dollar), as opposed to total sector-level impacts.
Direct impacts
Placeholder: impacts from a sector’s own operations and on-site processes.
Indirect impacts
Placeholder: impacts embodied in purchased inputs from other sectors (upstream supply chain).
Embodied impacts
Placeholder: direct plus indirect impacts associated with producing a sector’s output.
BEA / NAICS
Placeholder: Bureau of Economic Analysis sectoring aligned with North American Industry Classification System codes; see the sector reference table.
Total embodied impacts
Placeholder: aggregate embodied impacts for a sector or selection, often before normalizing by intensity.
Level of detail
Placeholder: simple (aggregated) versus detailed sector grouping in charts and tables.
